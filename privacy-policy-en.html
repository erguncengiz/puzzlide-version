Puzzlide - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Puzzlide ("we", "our", "the app"), developed by FE Labs, collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Puzzlide does not directly collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Game-related data is stored locally on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Game progress and statistics
  • Achievement progress
  • Language and theme preferences
  • Daily challenge streak data
  • High scores and personal records

3. Third-Party Services

Puzzlide uses the following third-party service:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect and process certain data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location data (country/region level)
  • App usage data and ad interaction data
  • Device information (model, operating system version)

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google uses data at Google Partner Sites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on iOS.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage entitlements (such as "No Ads"); RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase receipts and transaction data, device type and OS version. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Children's Privacy

Puzzlide is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown to users under 13 are served in compliance with applicable child privacy regulations (COPPA).

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties beyond what is described in the Third-Party Services section above. No game data stored on your device leaves your device.

6. Data Security

All game data is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. We do not operate servers that store your personal information. Ad-related data processing is handled by Google in accordance with their security standards.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of personalized ads via your device settings
  • Reset your Advertising Identifier at any time
  • Delete all local game data by uninstalling the app

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
